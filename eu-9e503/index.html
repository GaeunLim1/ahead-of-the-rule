EU 9E503.l and five national lists

A new EU control on technology for developing gas turbine axial compressors, checked against the US, UK, Japanese, Korean and Taiwanese lists.

As of 15 October 2026

1

The entry

Adopted on 14 September 2026. In force on publication in the Official Journal, after scrutiny ends around 14 November.

PartContent
ControlsTechnology required for the development of a gas turbine axial compressor
l.1, any ofMultiple stream fan systems; feedback control of blade tip clearance; feedback management against flutter; cooled gas path or rotating components; tandem or splittered rotating aerofoils; non-axisymmetric casing at the blade tip
l.2, all ofAverage alternate stage loading ≥ 0.22; 41.6 × loading + polytropic efficiency ≥ 102; stability margin ≥ 20%; average stage pressure ratio > 1.2
Not defined"Feedback" has no definition or technical note

The marked period is the shortest the gap can be. Korea has added EU 500 series entries that also had a US entry; this one has none.

Implication

Until another list adds it, the control applies only to transfers out of the EU, only EU exporters carry the licensing cost, and later lists are likely to start from its wording.

EffectWhy
ScopeEU law controls technology leaving the EU, not its re-export. The same technology leaves the US, UK, Japan or Korea without a licence
CostOnly EU senders and their partners carry the licensing burden
WordingThe next list to add it starts from this text, undefined "feedback" included. Korea's item 57.a already copies US 9E993.a word for word
