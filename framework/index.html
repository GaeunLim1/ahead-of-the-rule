THE FRAME

Identifying supply chain risk when export control rules change

A tool that connects a rule change to a firm's own transaction flow and finds where it needs to look again. Each finding is tagged by what it rests on.

See it applied to two rules on one transaction →

STEP 1

Identify the change

What changed, what it targets, and what fires it.

change · target · trigger

STEP 2

Place it on the transaction flow

Where the change reaches the firm's chain of exports and reexports.

export chain · what moves · who and through where · how far and when

STEP 3

Point to what needs a second look

What the firm's existing compliance programme has to check again.

classification · licensing · screening · release · records · training

STEP 4

Identify potential supply chain risk

Where a disruption can arise, and what it costs.

licensing · shipment · counterparty × time · labour · foregone

Overlapping rules Which other rules attach, and how do they relate?

How an overlapping rule relates

RelationMeaning
CumulativeEach answered on its own; clearing one does not clear the other
Same fact, two listsOne fact, two tests; answers can differ
HandoffOne rule creates the condition for the other
ConflictComplying with one breaches the other

3 · Point to what needs a second look

Check againWhen the change brings
ClassificationA new or amended list entry
Licence determinationA changed licence requirement or policy
Party screeningA new or extended party test
Red flags and catch-allA new knowledge trigger
Hold and releaseA change taking effect mid-transaction
Technology releaseA new controlled activity
Group instructionsA duty that falls on an affiliate
RecordsA new determination to evidence
TrainingAny of the above

4 · Identify potential supply chain risk

Where it arisesVariableQuestionCost
Licensing Licence path What routes to a lawful shipment remain? Labour · Foregone
Lead time How long until the firm can ship? Time · Foregone
Post-licensing conditions What conditions stay after the licence? Labour
Shipment Hold and release What makes a shipment wait? Time
Effective-date exposure What is in progress when it takes effect? Time · Foregone
Rerouting Does a new route meet another rule? Labour · Time
Counterparty Counterparty loss Who can the firm no longer deal with? Foregone
Replacement Does a substitute meet the same test? Labour · Foregone
