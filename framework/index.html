THE FRAME

A variable frame for identifying regulatory risk in a single rule

The frame breaks one export control rule into variables: those the rule carries and those that must be established where it attaches. From them it identifies where the rule can become a supply chain risk, and marks each finding by what it rests on: the text, the structure of the text, an assumption, or the chain itself.

1 · Target

TypeMeasured onExample
Attribute-based A continuous value measured on the item itself: content share, technical lineage, an aggregated count. A de minimis threshold on controlled US-origin content, measured by value; the calculated parameters written into a control list entry (e.g. 9E503.l.).
Relational A position in a network: nationality, headquarters location, a contract chain, a control relationship. Headquarters in a restricted jurisdiction; a re-export clause running through a third-country buyer; ownership of 50% or more by a listed entity, extending a listing to unlisted affiliates.
Conduct-based A future behavior the text conditions on, not a present property. A disciplinary commitment made as a condition of a suspended denial order; a duty to resolve a red flag or obtain a license once a party has knowledge of a listed owner.

2 · Range

Scope of authority

TypeMeaning
Within the existing lineStays inside the range already handed to the agency.
Redrawing the lineExtends the agency's own reach with a new jurisdictional or definitional line.

Cite the authorizing text and stated aim directly. A new class of actor that neither covers means the line is redrawn.

Trigger mechanism

TypeMeaning
Fixed triggerFires on a calendar date or another value no actor can change.
State triggerFires when a value measured on an item or party crosses a threshold. The value can move with no change in the rule.
Conduct triggerFires on a breach or a failure to perform a condition, leaving a latent interval before detection.

3 · Transfer chain

GroupVariableQuestion
What moves Event What counts as a further transfer: a cross-border movement, a change of end-user or end-use, a release of technology?
State What value does the text measure, and what can move it: an ownership share, a content share, a classification?
Where it attaches Party Whom does the text bind at each later transfer?
Edge Where does the text stop attaching: a stated condition, nowhere, or a new question at each transfer?
Layer Which other rules attach at each transfer, and how do they relate to this one?

How a layer relates

RelationMeaning
Separate reachAttaches to a different fact, answered on its own.
Same fact, two listsSame item or party against each rule's own list; the answers can differ.
HandoffWhat happens under one rule creates the condition for the other.

One rule is analyzed. Others appear as layers.

4 · Supply chain disruption

Identifies where the rule's structure can become supply chain risk, from the text of the rule rather than any one firm's chain. It does not score likelihood or impact.

GroupVariableQuestion
Where the rule bites Position Where does what the rule names sit in the supply chain, and does it stay there once the item moves on?
Substitutes Does the text leave a route around the line: an exception, an authorization path, an item outside the range, a point past the edge?
How long it stays unsettled Boundary Settlement Does the text settle what falls inside, and if not, does it name who can: a classification request, an advisory opinion, a licensing authority?
Response Window Is there time to respond: an interval before effect, a signal beforehand, or another rule's condition arriving on its own date?

What each finding rests on

TagMeaning
In the textStated in the article or its notes.
In the structure of the textFollows from how the text is built: where a definition sits, what it leaves out.
An assumptionNeeds a stated assumption about how an actor will behave.
Depends on the chainCannot be determined from the rule. Left to the firm that applies the frame: how many rules attach, how the measured value moves, the scale of dependency.

A variable that does not apply is recorded as not applicable. That is also a finding.
