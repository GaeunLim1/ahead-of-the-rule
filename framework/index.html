THE FRAME

Identifying supply chain risk when export control rules change

When a rule changes, the first question is what moved into scope. This frame breaks a rule into variables and marks where its structure becomes supply chain risk, each finding tagged by what it rests on. Where more than one rule reaches a transaction, each is read against the others.

1 · Target

TypeMeasured onExample
Item-based A value measured on the item itself: controlled content share, technical lineage, a calculated parameter. A de minimis threshold on controlled US-origin content, measured by value; the calculated parameters written into a control list entry (e.g. 9E503.l.).
Party-based Who the party is or who it is tied to: nationality, headquarters location, a contract chain, an ownership relationship. Headquarters in a restricted jurisdiction; a re-export clause running through a third-country buyer; ownership of 50% or more by a listed entity, extending a listing to unlisted affiliates.
Conduct-based A future behavior the text conditions on, not a present property. A disciplinary commitment made as a condition of a suspended denial order; a duty to resolve a red flag or obtain a licence once a party has knowledge of a listed owner.

2 · Range

Scope of authority

TypeMeaning
Within the existing lineStays inside the range already handed to the agency.
Redrawing the lineExtends the agency's own reach with a new jurisdictional or definitional line.

Cite the authorizing text and stated aim directly. A new class of actor that neither covers means the line is redrawn.

Trigger mechanism

TypeMeaning
Fixed triggerFires on a calendar date or another value no actor can change.
State triggerFires when a value measured on an item or party crosses a threshold. The value can move with no change in the rule.
Conduct triggerFires on a breach or a failure to perform a condition, leaving a latent interval before detection.

3 · Transfer chain

GroupVariableQuestion
What moves Controlled activity What counts as a further export: a re-export, a transfer, transit, brokering, technical assistance, an electronic transmission of technology? Does a firm's own rerouting or re-sourcing create one?
Measured value What value does the text measure (technical characteristics, an ownership share, a content share), what can move it, and can the firm see that value at each hop?
Where it attaches Exporter Whom does the text bind at each later transfer: the exporter (who holds the contract with the consignee and can determine the sending), a broker, a technical assistance provider? Can one entity be bound by more than one regime at once?
Where it stops Where does the text stop attaching: a stated condition, nowhere, or a new question at each transfer?
Overlapping rules Which other rules attach at each transfer: a second control list (EU and national), embargoed, sanctioned or sensitive destinations and entities, tariffs? How do they relate to this one?

How an overlapping rule relates

RelationMeaning
Separate reachAttaches to a different fact, answered on its own.
Same fact, two listsSame item or party against each rule's own list (for example EU and national control lists); the answers can differ.
HandoffWhat happens under one rule creates the condition for the other.
ConflictComplying with one rule breaches the other.

Each rule is analyzed on its own. Where others reach the same transaction, they appear as overlapping rules.

4 · Supply chain disruption

Identifies where the rule's structure can become supply chain risk, from the text of the rule rather than any one firm's chain. It does not score likelihood or impact.

GroupVariableQuestion
Where the rule bites Position in the chain Where does what the rule names sit in the supply chain, how concentrated is the dependence on it, and does it stay there once the item moves on?
Workarounds Does the text leave a route around the line: an exception, a general or global authorisation, an item outside the range, a point past the edge? If the firm re-sources or reroutes instead, does the new route meet another rule?
Post-licensing controls Once an authorisation is held, does the text attach conditions that stay with the item or the holder (shipment control, the conditions of the authorisation), and can a breach, or a change in the holder's details, suspend it, void it or hold a shipment?
How long it stays unsettled Scope certainty Does the text settle what falls inside, and if not, does it name who can: technical classification advice, an advisory opinion, a licensing authority?
Catch-all and diversion risk Does the text turn on being informed by the competent authority, or on red flags of diversion risk, and does it settle what triggers the duty to notify the authority or to apply for an authorisation?
Response window Is there time to respond: an interval before effect, a signal beforehand, another rule's condition arriving on its own date, a rule that can be suspended, reinstated or reversed? How long does it take to translate a change in the lists into the firm's classification procedures?

What each finding rests on

TagMeaning
In the textStated in the article or its notes.
In the structure of the textFollows from how the text is built: where a definition sits, what it leaves out.
An assumptionNeeds a stated assumption about how an actor will behave.
Depends on the chainCannot be determined from the rule. Left to the firm that applies the frame: how many rules attach, how the measured value moves, the scale of dependency.

A variable that does not apply is recorded as not applicable. That is also a finding.
