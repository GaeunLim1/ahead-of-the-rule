RelationMeaning

4 · Identify potential supply-chain risk

Rests on the rules for licence path and dates, and on the firm's chain for the rest.

Where it arisesVariableQuestionBurden type
Licensing Licence path What routes to a lawful shipment remain? Labour · Foregone
Lead time How long until the firm can ship? Time · Foregone
Post-licensing conditions What conditions stay after the licence? Labour
Shipment Hold and release What makes a shipment wait? Time
Effective-date exposure What is in progress when it takes effect? Time · Foregone
Rerouting Does a new route meet another rule? Labour · Time
Counterparty Counterparty loss Who can the firm no longer deal with? Foregone
