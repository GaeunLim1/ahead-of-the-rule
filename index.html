The Affiliates Rule, traced as a rule

One rule, put through a fixed frame. What it names, how far it reaches, where it attaches, and where it can hold up a chain. Each finding is tagged by what it rests on.

THE CHAIN THIS RULE IS TRACED ALONG US export EU reexport third country owner unresolved Each hop assessed on the facts at that point. The rule asks who owns the recipient, not what the item is.

WHERE THIS READING STOPS

The rule names one "exporter, reexporter, or transferor." Inside a multinational that actor is several legal entities, and the knowledge the rule turns on can sit in one while the transaction runs through another. Whether a group's knowledge is held where the duty attaches is not in the text. It is a chain variable, traced in the companion post on knowledge distribution.
