THE FRAMEWORK

Where the Rules Meet: A Compliance Impact Framework

For one export route, see which rules attach, where they meet, and what to check again before a new rule takes effect.

Covers export controls, sanctions, and the tariff basics an exporter handles. Not covered: duty calculation, valuation, customs procedures, or import-country regulation.

How rules relate

RelationMeaning
CumulativeEach answered on its own; clearing one does not clear the other
Same fact, two listsOne fact, two tests; answers can differ
HandoffOne rule creates the condition for the other
ConflictComplying with one breaches the other
IncentiveOne rule gives a reason to change the route or origin that another rule reads
