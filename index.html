WORKED ANALYSIS · TWO RULES, ONE TRANSACTION

When two new rules reach the same shipment

Two changes, read side by side. The EU adds Dual-Use entry 9E503.l. on development technology for gas turbine engine axial compressors. The US brings back the BIS Affiliates Rule on who owns the recipient. Both are expected to apply from November 2026.

THE TWO RULES ON THIS TRANSACTION EU 9E503.l. · the item US Affiliates Rule · the party Different targets, same shipment. One is read on what the item is; the other on who owns the recipient.

1 · Identify the change

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Type of change New: technology not controlled before is now in scope. Expanded: listings reach affiliates owned 50% or more.
Where it sits A new control list entry. A standard applied across existing lists, not a new listing. In the structure
Instrument Commission Delegated Regulation amending Annex I Interim final rule
Dates Adopted 14 September 2026. Applies from publication, expected November 2026, date not fixed. Suspended until 9 November 2026; returns 10 November 2026.
Target Item: development technology Party: who owns the recipient
Trigger State: a technical threshold State: 50% ownership. Knowledge: Red Flag 29

2 · Place it on the transaction flow

Assumed chain: an EU entity develops the technology, which includes US-origin technology, and transmits it to an affiliate outside the Union. The affiliate uses it to supply a customer. An assumption

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Type An export from the Union A reexport, if the technology is subject to the EAR
Change in between Either rule can take effect between the transmission and the affiliate's next reexport.
Controlled activity Export, including electronic transmission. Export, reexport or transfer where an affiliate is a party.
Determining fact Technical specification. Two design features undefined (l.1.b.1, l.1.b.2). Data sits inside the firm. Ownership share. Data sits outside the firm.
Parties Binds the exporter. Screens no party. Binds the exporter or reexporter. Reads every party for a listed owner.
Route Not applicable Not applicable as a route test
Carry-over Stops at the Union's border. In the structure Follows items subject to the EAR. In the structure
Checkpoint At release At order and before each shipment
Overlapping rules Cumulative: clearing one does not clear the other. In the structure

Where the two rules meet

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
What it reads Technical data Ownership
Who in the firm reads it Engineering, classification Screening
Where the data sits Inside the firm Outside the firm
Who grants the licence The Member State authority BIS

The two rules are cleared by different teams, on different data, by different authorities. A shipment or a transmission leaves only when both are cleared, so its timing is set by whichever comes last. In the structure

4 · Identify potential supply chain risk

Lead time, post-licensing conditions, costs and how far each spreads depend on the firm's chain.

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Licence path Individual or global authorisation. Just outside the parameters, hands off to the Article 4 catch-all. In the structure Licence on the listed owner's terms; the most restrictive owner applies.
Lead time Classification advice on the two undefined features, then licensing. Ownership must be established first; open-ended if the review policy is restrictive.
Post-licensing conditions Set by the authorisation. An ownership change can change the answer later.
Hold and release Release may be held while classification is open. An assumption Cannot proceed until Red Flag 29 is resolved.
Effective-date exposure Applies from publication, no transition. Applies from 10 November 2026.
Rerouting Not applicable Not applicable
Counterparty loss Only where a licence is refused for a destination. The recipient and every entity it owns 50%+, suppliers included. In the structure
Replacement A substitute in the same destination meets the same entry. In the structure A substitute meets the same ownership test. In the structure
What it costs Time: classification and licensing. Labour: classification at each release. Foregone: markets where refused. Time: ownership checks and licensing. Labour: ownership data per party, per shipment. Foregone: counterparties inside the 50% set.
How far it spreads If the technology is on a critical path, the chain waits. If the recipient is on a critical path, the chain waits.
