WORKED ANALYSIS · TWO RULES, ONE TRANSACTION

When two new rules reach the same shipment

Two recent changes, read side by side. The EU adds a Dual-Use entry on compressor development technology (9E503.l.); the US adds the BIS Affiliates Rule on who owns the recipient. A shipment that was clear under both yesterday can be caught by each today.

THE TWO RULES ON THIS TRANSACTION EU 9E503.l. · the item US Affiliates Rule · the party Different targets, same shipment. One is read on what the item is; the other on who owns the recipient.

1 · Target of the rule

TypeEU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Item-based  Compressor development technology.
Party-based  Who owns the recipient (50%+ by a listed entity).
Conduct-based  A duty that opens on knowledge of a listed owner.

The two rules aim at different things. One reads the item; the other reads the party. On one shipment, both apply.

2 · Range of the rule

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
What changed A new entry. Technology that was not controlled is now in scope. In the text A new 50% standard. Reach extends past the listed entity to the affiliate it owns. In the structure
Trigger A numeric threshold. No conduct condition. The fact of 50%+ ownership (strict liability), and knowledge (Red Flag 29).

Both are recent additions. Each puts something in scope that a firm did not have to watch before.

3 · Transfer chain

VariableEU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Measured value Technical specification. Two design features carry no definition (l.1.b.1, l.1.b.2). Ownership percentage.
Party bound Whoever develops or transfers the technology. The exporter, reexporter, or transferor.
Overlapping rules A shipment that is a US export and an onward reexport meets both: the item against the EU list, the party against the US list. Same transaction, two regimes, assessed on their own facts. In the structure

This is where the two rules actually meet.

What waits If that technology sits on a critical path, the chain waits with it. If the recipient sits on a critical path, the chain waits with it.

Two rules, two different reasons, one chain. Either can hold the same shipment, and each is unsettled in its own way.
