The Affiliates Rule, analyzed as a rule

The frame applied to one rule, section by section. Findings stated in the text carry no tag; a tag marks the ones that rest on the structure of the text, an assumption, or the chain.

TypeThis ruleRests on

Conduct-based

A control relationship (50%+ ownership, extended to the affiliate) and a duty that opens on knowledge. No value measured on the item.

2 · Range of the rule

Scope of authority

Redrawing the line. In the structure  § 744.11 reaches the unlisted affiliate, a party the list never named.

Trigger mechanism

Three triggers. State (50%+ ownership, strict liability), conduct (Red Flag 29, on knowledge), and a fixed date (the 60-day TGL).

4 · Supply chain disruption

VariableOn this ruleRests on
Position in the chain On the party, not the item. A clean item could still be held by its recipient. In the structure
Workarounds Resolve, license, or exception. All close at once if ownership stays unresolved.

Response window Fires with no prior signal. Only the 60-day TGL as buffer.
