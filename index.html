1 · Analyze the change

Rests on the text of the rule, before and after the change, and the official record of when it applies.

The change

VariableQuestion
RuleWhich rule changes, and how: new, expanded, narrowed, clarified, or removed?
Whose ruleThe exporter's home country, a country whose rule follows the item onward, or the destination? Agreed in a multilateral regime, or added by one country or a few?
Status and datesAnnounced, adopted, under scrutiny, published, in force, or stayed? Which date is fixed in law, which is only expected, and is there a transition?

The line

BoundaryCompare before and after
WhoWhich parties are inside the rule, including through ownership, and which are carved out?
WhatWhich items, software or technology, and which are carved out?
Which actsBeyond export, reexport, transfer and release, does it reach dealing, technical assistance, or brokering?
ThresholdAt what value, share, specification or defined term does it fire? Is every term the line relies on defined, and if not, how do other lists word the same thing?
License termsIs a license required, under what review policy, and which license exceptions remain?

2 · Place it on the transaction

Rests on the transaction you set. An assumption unless the deal is real.

The transaction

VariableCovers
Itemtype (product, part, material, software, technology) · which rules it is subject to (where it is made or developed, where it is sent from, controlled content against de minimis, foreign direct product) · its classification under each (an entry, or not listed) · who holds the data needed to classify it
Partiesparticipants, including suppliers, contract designers and manufacturers, and anyone who receives technology · ownership, tier by tier · the lists each party is screened against · what can change before the deal is done
Route and channellegs, and whether each still happens · actor · terms · who designs and who makes (build to print, or design and make) · how technology moves (file, server access, visit, remote support), and in which direction, including back
Timingorder · shipment, read under each date the change sets

Where it lands

PointWhere on the routeRead by
ExportThe item leaves the exporter’s countryThe exporter
ReexportThe item moves on from one foreign country to anotherThe reexporter
TransferThe end user or end use changes within one countryThe transferor
ReleaseTechnology is shown, sent or explained to a personThe provider
ReceiptThe item or technology arrivesThe recipient, through what it signs: an end-user statement, an import certificate, and any condition on passing it on

Position

PositionMeaning
Near the lineJust inside or just outside; a small change in a stake, a figure or the reading of a term moves it across
Moves mid-transactionThe line holds still; a fact (a stake, a listing, an end use) moves across it
Depends on the dateInside under one date, outside under another

3 · Comply with both

Rests on the text of the exporter’s own country’s rule, and on the change and the transaction above.

VariableQuestion
ReachWhat does each rule catch on this transaction, through its control list, a list tied to destinations, or a catch-all? What does only one of them catch?
TimingWhen does each apply? Does one move while the other holds still?
Separate stepsWhich licenses, filings and documents does each require on its own? Does clearing one clear the other?
Both at onceCan the firm meet both, or does complying with one breach the other?

4 · Turn it into action

Rests on the sections above, and on the compliance guidance in the sources.

VariableQuestion
Checks to redoWhich classifications, screenings and ownership traces must be done again, which data must come from suppliers to do them, and at which point before the item leaves?
Date exposureWhat is in progress when it takes effect? If the date is not settled, which date does the firm plan on, and what does a wrong guess cost?
License pathWhat paths to a lawful shipment remain, how long until the firm can ship, and which license clears last?
ResponseDoes holding, re-timing or restructuring the shipment meet another rule, or start to look like circumvention?
Contract and recordsWhat was agreed before the rule applies, and which terms put the risk on the firm? What does the firm record of each decision?
