EU DUAL-USE · 9E503 · ONE DESIGN LOOP

The EU’s New Control on Compressor Technology, on One Design Loop: A Review

Adopted on 14 September 2026 and under scrutiny. Followed around one design loop between a Korean ODM and an EU parent.

THE LOOP IN THIS REVIEW Korea · ODM designs EU · parent reviews Korea · subsidiary, ODM

1 · Analyze the change

Rests on the text of the Annex to Commission Delegated Regulation C(2026) 6323, and the Commission’s notice of its adoption.

The change

VariableEU 9E503
RuleNew entry 9E503.l: development technology. Annex I, Regulation (EU) 2021/821.
Whose ruleThe EU. Regime origin not stated.
Status and datesAdopted 14 September 2026; two-month scrutiny; in force on publication in the Official Journal. No date as of 12 October 2026.

The line

BoundaryBeforeAfter
WhoAny exporter from the EUUnchanged
What—Development technology for a gas turbine axial compressor with any one l.1 feature (multiple stream fan; feedback control of blade tip clearance; feedback management against flutter; cooled components; tandem or splittered aerofoils; non-axisymmetric casings) and all l.2 parameters
Which actsExport, including electronic transmissionUnchanged
Threshold—Stage loading ≥ 0.22; stage loading × 41.6 + polytropic efficiency ≥ 102; stability margin ≥ 20%; stage pressure ratio > 1.2
Defined terms—“Development”: all phases before serial production, including design data. “Required”: only the part peculiarly responsible for the controlled levels.
Terms left open—“Feedback” (l.1.b.1, l.1.b.2): not defined. Korea’s Annex 2-2 item 57.a uses “active compensating casing”.
License terms—Authorisation required. EU007: to a wholly owned subsidiary in Korea, if not shared with another entity. Otherwise individual or global.

2 · Place it on the transaction

Rests on an assumption. Set for this review, not a fact from the rules.

The transaction

VariableThis transaction
ItemDevelopment technology for an industrial gas turbine axial compressor. Assumed: meets l.2; has tip clearance control and flutter management. Classification: 9E503.l if either is feedback. Data: held by the ODM.
PartiesAn EU gas turbine maker (the parent); its wholly owned Korean subsidiary; a Korean ODM that designs and builds the compressor. None is listed. What can move: the design.
Route and channelLeg 1: ODM to parent, through the subsidiary, for review (Korea to the EU). Leg 2: parent returns comments and revised data by server download (the EU to Korea). Leg 3: subsidiary to ODM (within Korea).
TimingMonthly design reviews, November 2026 to March 2027. Publication in the Official Journal: not fixed. Korea’s amendment: not announced.

Where it lands

LegPointWhere the line lands
Leg 1: Korea to the EUKorea: export. EU: arrival.Does not land.
Leg 2: the EU to KoreaEU: export, by the parent. Receipt by the subsidiary (end-user statement).Lands from publication, on “required” data.
Leg 3: within KoreaWithin Korea.Does not land. Breaks EU007’s no-sharing condition.

3 · Comply with both

Rests on the text of the EU Annex I entry, Korea’s Public Notice on Export and Import of Strategic Items (Annex 2 and Annex 2-2, as of 12 October 2026), and this review’s assumptions.

VariableEU: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, 9E503Korea: Foreign Trade Act, Art. 19
ReachLeg 2.No Annex 2 entry for this technology. Annex 2-2 item 57.a: tip clearance control technology, Russia and Belarus only. General catch-all.
TimingFrom publication; not before mid-November 2026.Not announced. Earlier EU-led 5xx entries (3A501, 4A506) already added.
Separate stepsIndividual or global authorisation covering the ODM (EU007 unavailable). End-user statement from the subsidiary.None.
Both at onceNo conflict. Until Korea amends, the subsidiary is bound only by its EU end-user statement.

4 · Turn it into action

Rests on the sections above, and on the compliance guidance in the sources.

VariableIn this transaction
Checks to redoClassify against 9E503.l before the first review after publication, and at each design change. From the ODM: l.2 values; how clearance control and flutter management work.
Date exposurePlan on mid-November: reviews may wait; the programme slips. Plan later: data may return without an authorisation.
License pathIndividual or global authorisation for Leg 2. Until granted, return only data that is not “required”, and record the judgement.
ResponseReviewing in Korea does not avoid it if data is sent from the EU. Moving design into the subsidiary opens EU007; record why.
Contract and recordsODM: classification data, notice of design changes. Subsidiary: end-user statement terms. Records: each transfer’s date and classification; the reading of “feedback”.
