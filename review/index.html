EU 9E503.l. × US AFFILIATES RULE

Two Export Control Rules on One Transaction: A Review

The EU adds Dual-Use entry 9E503.l. on technology for developing gas turbine compressors. The US brings back the Affiliates Rule on who owns the recipient. Both are expected to apply from November 2026. Here they meet on one transaction.

THE TWO RULES ON THIS TRANSACTION EU 9E503.l. · the item US Affiliates Rule · the party

0 · Define the transaction

Rests on an assumption. Set for this review, not a fact from the rules.

 This transaction
Item Technology for developing gas turbine compressors.
Chain One export of the technology from an EU firm to a recipient in a third country. The third country’s own controls are outside this review.
Parties The EU exporter, and a recipient owned 50% or more, through an intermediate company, by a listed party. The technology is subject to the EAR.
Route Direct, by electronic transmission from the Union. The destination is outside the destinations of EU001.
Timing Both rules are expected to apply from November 2026. Either can take effect before the export leaves.

2 · See where they meet

Rests on the text of the rules and how they are built. Who reads each and where the data sits rest on the assumed chain.

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Type An export from the Union, by electronic transmission. A reexport of technology subject to the EAR.
Controlled activity Export, including electronic transmission. Export, reexport or transfer (in-country) where an affiliate is a party.
What it reads Technical specification. Two design features undefined (l.1.b.1, l.1.b.2). Ownership share, traced through the intermediate company.
Parties Binds the exporter. Ownership is not read. Binds the exporter or reexporter. Reads every party for a listed owner; the recipient is caught through its parent.
Carry-over Applies directly up to the Union’s border; beyond it, only through licence conditions. Follows items subject to the EAR.
Checkpoint At release At order and before each shipment
Who in the firm reads it Engineering, classification Screening
Where the data sits Inside the firm Outside the firm
Who grants the licence The Member State authority BIS
Relation Cumulative: clearing one does not clear the other.
Clears last The two rules are cleared by different teams, on different data, by different authorities. A shipment or a transmission leaves only when both are cleared, so its timing is set by whichever comes last.

4 · Identify potential supply-chain risk

Rests on the rules for licence path and dates; the firm’s chain for lead time, conditions, burdens and how far each spreads.

 EU 9E503.l.US Affiliates Rule
Licence path Individual or global authorisation; EU001 does not cover the destination. Just outside the parameters, hands off to the Article 4 catch-all. Licence on the listed owner’s terms; the most restrictive owner applies.
Lead time Classification advice on the two undefined features, then licensing. Ownership must be established first; open-ended if the review policy is restrictive.
Post-licensing conditions Set by the authorisation. An ownership change can change the answer later.
Hold and release Release may be held while classification is open. Cannot proceed until Red Flag 29 is resolved.
Effective-date exposure Applies from entry into force; the Annex sets no transition. Applies from 10 November 2026.
Rerouting Not applicable Not applicable
Counterparty loss Only where a licence is refused for a destination. The recipient and every entity it owns 50%+, suppliers included.
Replacement A substitute in the same destination meets the same entry. A substitute meets the same ownership test.
Burden type Time: classification and licensing. Labour: classification at each release. Foregone: markets where refused. Time: ownership checks and licensing. Labour: ownership data per party, per shipment. Foregone: counterparties inside the 50% set.
How far it spreads If the technology is on a critical path, the chain waits. If the recipient is on a critical path, the chain waits.
