The case in one view

LensEU 9E503.lKorea
ScopeDevelopment technology for a qualifying gas-turbine axial compressor.No 9E503 entry in supplied Annex 2; Annex 2-2 item 57.a separately covers narrower tip-clearance technology for Russia and Belarus.
BoundaryOne l.1 feature and all l.2 performance parameters.57.a uses “active compensating casing” and limits the technology to a design and development database.
TransferEU outbound technology transfer, including electronic provision and oral transmission.Situation-permit question depends on Korean item scope, destination, and any applicable exemption.
StatusAdopted 14 September 2026; effective date depends on scrutiny and Official Journal publication.Current strategic list and situation list are separate checks; later amendment remains possible.

The operating-model comparison

Decision pointOEM / in-houseODM / external design
EU routeEU parent or supplier sends technology to a Korean entity.EU sender may provide data directly to an ODM, or the Korean entity may pass it onward.
AuthorisationEU007 is possible only if the parties and use meet its conditions.Independent ODM access may fall outside EU007’s no-sharing / group conditions; check the actual licence route.
Korean exporterUsually holds the design evidence needed for classification.May lack the ODM’s feedback details or performance calculations needed to classify.

OEM / ODM is a design-and-manufacture model. It does not, by itself, determine the licence or permit.

Three open edges

Boundary

“Feedback” is undefined; the 9E maintenance-note omission leaves the relationship with the General Technology Note open.

Timing

EU adoption is known; publication and effective date must be checked. Korean list alignment may follow later.

Accountability

The Korean exporter may need technical data and EU licence terms held by its parent, supplier, or ODM.

Key insight
The gap is where classification evidence, the EU licence chain, and Korean destination controls meet.
