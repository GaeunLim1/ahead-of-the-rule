US AFFILIATES RULE · ONE EXPORT ROUTE

The US Affiliates Rule on One Export Route: A Review

The US Affiliates Rule reaches recipients through who owns them. Its stay ends on 10 November 2026 in law; an extension to 10 January 2027 has been announced but not published. Here it is followed through one export route from Korea under both dates, leg by leg, to where it reaches the supply chain.

THE ROUTE IN THIS REVIEW Korea · exporter Country X · C Country Y · D

1 · Read the change

Rests on the text of the rule, before and after the change, and the official record of when it applies.

The change

VariableUS Affiliates Rule
RuleExpanded: an existing set of listings reaches further. Interim final rule.
Whose ruleA country whose rule follows the item: the US. Added by the US alone, not agreed in a regime.
Status and datesAdopted and stayed. Fixed in law: the stay ends on 9 November 2026 and the rule returns on 10 November 2026. Announced: on 23 September 2026 the US and China agreed to extend the Busan arrangement to 10 January 2027, expected to carry the stay with it. No Federal Register notice of the extension found as of 1 October 2026.

The line

BoundaryBeforeAfter
WhoParties named on the Entity List or the MEU List, and branches not legally distinct from themAlso foreign entities owned 50% or more by them, or by parties designated under certain SDN programmes, directly or indirectly, individually or in aggregate. This review follows Entity List owners only.
WhatItems subject to the EAR, per each entryUnchanged
Which actsNothing beyond export, reexport and in-country transferUnchanged
ThresholdNone for ownership50%, counted again at each tier
Licence termsPer entry, for the listed party onlyThe affiliate takes the most restrictive owner’s licence requirement, review policy and licence exception eligibility

Two boundaries moved, who and the threshold, and the licence terms follow from them. What is not settled is the date.

2 · Place it on the transaction

Rests on an assumption. Set for this review, not a fact from the rules. The ownership structure is Application example 1 in the text of the rule.

The transaction

VariableThis transaction
ItemA part made for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, made in Korea. US-origin content is above the de minimis level, so the part is subject to the EAR.
PartiesA Korean exporter. Company A is on the Entity List, needs a licence for all items subject to the EAR, and has a presumption of denial; it owns 35% of C. Company B is on the Entity List, needs a licence for items on the CCL, and is reviewed case by case; it owns 15% of C. C owns 50% of D. Neither C nor D is listed. What can move: a stake in C or D, or the listing of A, B or another shareholder.
Route and channelLeg 1: Korea to Country X, to C as distributor. Leg 2: Country X to Country Y, to D as end user, which happens only if the part leaves Korea. Goods only; no technology moves.
TimingOrder placed in October 2026. Shipment planned for December 2026. Payment due in February 2027. Read under two dates: 10 November 2026 (fixed in law) and 10 January 2027 (announced).

Where it lands

LegPointWhere the line lands
Leg 1: Korea to XExport, read by the Korean exporterLands here. C is inside: A and B together own 50%. The exporter knows D is the end user, so D is read here too.
Leg 2: X to YReexport, read by CLands here. D is inside: C owns 50%.

Position

PositionIn this transaction
Out to inC and D, from the day the rule returns
Just insideBoth. C at exactly 50% in aggregate, D at exactly 50%. A small sale by A, B or C moves them out.
Just outsideA stake of 49%
Moves mid-transactionIf A is delisted before shipment, C falls to 15% and moves out, and D with it. If another shareholder of C is listed, C stays in even if A or B sells down.
Depends on the dateThe December shipment is inside if the rule returns on 10 November, outside if the stay runs to 10 January. The February payment falls inside under both.

4 · Where the chain ends

Rests on the chain in Step 3; the assumed supply chain for lead time, burdens and how far each spreads.
