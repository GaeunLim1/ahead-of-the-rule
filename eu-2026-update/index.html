EU 2026 update

Delegated Regulation C(2026) 6323 reaches the data centre at two points: 3A501.a.16.b on ICs designed for data centres, and 9E503.l on technology for developing gas turbine axial compressors.

Firm impact

A Korean "not controlled" result does not settle how the EU or the US classifies the same item.

ItemContent
High exposureBuyers of data centre ICs and servers from EU suppliers; co-development partners of EU gas turbine makers
InformationIC design purpose and parameters, held by the maker; compressor parameters, held by the developer
CostEU licences for each transfer out of the EU; changed lead times; classification in each jurisdiction separately
AllocationThe EU exporter carries the licence. The buyer supplies end-use and design-purpose information by contract
No contract fixesIf the buyer's own country adds the item later, two sets of obligations apply
