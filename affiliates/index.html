US Affiliates Rule

BIS extends Entity List and MEU restrictions to companies 50% or more owned by listed parties. In a data centre, it matters less for the chips than for everything around them: advanced chips already need a licence for firms headquartered in China.

Firm impact

A data centre's owner, not its name, decides whether its suppliers need a licence.

ItemContent
High exposureSuppliers of servers, network equipment and facility parts to data centres 50% or more owned by listed parties
InformationThe data centre's ownership chain, held by the customer
CostOwnership tracing, built once; licences when ownership is unclear; deals deferred until 10 Jan 2027
AllocationThe exporter is liable. Ownership disclosure and change-notice clauses move the information, not the liability
